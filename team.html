Team Work

Group F worked across different countries and schedules to produce the Unit 6 design proposal: Ruwan Perera, Isaac Shalaan, Tugba Unal and Luis Torres Zambrano.

How the idea changed

Stage What happened Evidence
Original concept A thesis-planning system using an LLM planner and three edge small-language-model agents Described in Isaac Shalaan’s memo
Challenge Isaac argued edge inference added hardware complexity without removing the network dependency, and proposed an Academic Evidence Investigator Proposal memo
Draft (23 August 2026) Five agents, with a separate Critic Team report drafts v3 and v4
Submitted design Four agents, analysis and critique merged; BDI mapping and CrewAI comparison added Unit 6 team report v2
Individual implementation Built by Ruwan in Unit 11 against the submitted design README and presentation

Peer evaluation

The peer evaluation submitted at the end of the team project rated Isaac 5 on every criterion, Tugba 4 to 5 and Luis 3 to 5, describing the team as well organised and constructively challenging, with one member’s tangent brought back on track. The self-evaluation totalled 29 of 35, noting one missed meeting.
