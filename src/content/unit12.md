# Unit 12: The Future of Intelligent Agents

| Direction | What is happening | Open questions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LLM-based agents | Models given tools, memory and planning loops act for users (Wang et al., 2024) | Reliability, hallucination, accountability |
| Multi-agent LLM systems | Many agents cooperate or simulate societies (Park et al., 2023) | Shared meaning, trust, emergent behaviour |
| Robot foundation models | Vision-language-action models map instructions to actions (Brohan et al., 2023) | Physical safety |
| Neuro-symbolic AI | Neural perception combined with symbolic reasoning (Garcez and Lamb, 2023) | Scalability |
| Safety and governance | Calls for oversight of advanced AI (Bengio et al., 2024); EU AI Act obligations phasing in | Enforcement, international coordination |

The Academic Evidence Investigator sits in the first of these: an LLM-based agent whose trustworthiness came less from the model than from the conventional software around it, such as validation, bounded loops, grounding and audit trails.
