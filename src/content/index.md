# Intelligent Agents E-Portfolio

::: kicker
Module portfolio
:::

::: card
This e-portfolio collects the evidence of my work on the Intelligent Agents module: weekly activities and agent implementations, contributions to the collaborative discussions, the Group F team design, my individual implementation of that design, three reflective case studies and a final reflection.

The central project is the **Academic Evidence Investigator**, an LLM-powered multi-agent system that plans a literature search, retrieves papers from Semantic Scholar and Crossref, appraises the evidence and writes a referenced research briefing, searching again when the evidence is thin or one-sided.
