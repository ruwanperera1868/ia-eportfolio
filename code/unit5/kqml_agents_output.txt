1. Direct query answered from beliefs
  buyer -> (ask-one :sender buyer :receiver warehouse :reply-with q1 :language KIF :ontology warehouse-v1 :content (stock Laptop))
  warehouse -> (tell :sender warehouse :receiver buyer :in-reply-to q1 :language KIF :ontology warehouse-v1 :content (= (stock Laptop) 12))

2. Query about a superclass, answered by reasoning over the ontology
  buyer -> (ask-one :sender buyer :receiver warehouse :reply-with q2 :language KIF :ontology warehouse-v1 :content (stock Electronics))
  warehouse -> (tell :sender warehouse :receiver buyer :in-reply-to q2 :language KIF :ontology warehouse-v1 :content (= (stock Electronics) 12))

3. A term outside the shared ontology
  buyer -> (ask-one :sender buyer :receiver warehouse :reply-with q3 :language KIF :ontology warehouse-v1 :content (stock Tablet))
  warehouse -> (sorry :sender warehouse :receiver buyer :in-reply-to q3 :comment "Tablet or stock not in ontology")

4. A request to act (achieve) rather than to inform
  buyer -> (achieve :sender buyer :receiver warehouse :reply-with q4 :language KIF :ontology warehouse-v1 :content (stock Phone))
  warehouse -> (tell :sender warehouse :receiver buyer :in-reply-to q4 :language KIF :ontology warehouse-v1 :content (= (stock Phone) 10))

5. Same words, different ontology: communication fails
  buyer2 -> (ask-one :sender buyer2 :receiver warehouse :reply-with q1 :language KIF :ontology retail-v2 :content (stock Laptop))
  warehouse -> (sorry :sender warehouse :receiver buyer2 :in-reply-to q1 :comment "unknown ontology")
